1. Match: does the data deliver what the LISTING claims? If not, it is worth 0.
2. Specificity: can it be acted on as written, or is it vague? Vague claims are worth 0.
3. Verifiability: can your principal confirm it, for example by reproducing an exploit step by step?
4. Impact: what loss does it prevent, or what gain does it enable, against the amount your principal has at risk?
5. Exclusivity: is it likely to be already known or public? Public information is worth 0.

Value it at a small fraction of the impact. Never value it above the impact.
